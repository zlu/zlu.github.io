**Data** are the realised numerical values $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ once a [random sample](/cs-glossary/random-sample/) has been drawn. Before observation, $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are random; after observation, $x$ is fixed and is plugged into formulas to produce an [estimate](/cs-glossary/estimate/).

Do not call a number like $\bar x=5.2$ an "estimator" — the estimator is the rule $\bar X$; $5.2$ is an estimate computed from data.
