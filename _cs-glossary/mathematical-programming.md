**Mathematical programming** formulates decisions as an optimisation problem: choose [decision variables](/cs-glossary/decision-variable/) to optimise an [objective](/cs-glossary/objective-function/) subject to [constraints](/cs-glossary/constraint/).

Families: [linear](/cs-glossary/linear-programming/) (LP), [integer](/cs-glossary/integer-programming/) (IP/MIP), nonlinear, network flows. Transport uses: fleet sizing, routing, assignment, flows.
