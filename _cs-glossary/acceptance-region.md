The **acceptance region** $R^c$ is the complement of the [critical (rejection) region](/cs-glossary/critical-region/): the set of sample outcomes for which we *do not* reject $H_0$.

“Accept” is classical jargon for “fail to reject” — it does not prove $H_0$. Equivalent rule: [$p$-value](/cs-glossary/p-value/) $>\alpha$.
