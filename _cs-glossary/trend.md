A **trend** is the long-run level movement of a [time series](/cs-glossary/time-series/) — often modelled as a linear function of time, $Y_t=b_0+b_1 t+u_t$, or absorbed by [differencing](/cs-glossary/differencing/).

Naive forecasts may use trend alone; richer models add [seasonality](/cs-glossary/seasonality/) and covariates. Stochastic trends need unit-root / ARIMA thinking ([stationarity](/cs-glossary/stationarity/)).
