A **Bernoulli**($p$) [random variable](/cs-glossary/random-variable/) takes value $1$ with probability $p$ and $0$ with probability $1-p$. Mean $p$, variance $p(1-p)$. The [MLE](/cs-glossary/maximum-likelihood/) / [method of moments](/cs-glossary/method-of-moments/) estimator of $p$ from an [iid](/cs-glossary/iid/) sample is the [sample mean](/cs-glossary/sample-mean/) $\bar X$.

$n$ iid Bernoulli trials yield a Binomial count; indicator variables for events are Bernoulli.
