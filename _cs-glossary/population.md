The **population** is the complete set of units (or the probability model) about which we want to learn. Unknown [parameters](/cs-glossary/parameter/) $\theta$ describe the population distribution $f_{X\mid\theta}$.

A [random sample](/cs-glossary/random-sample/) is drawn from the population; [inference](/cs-glossary/statistical-inference/) uses the sample to estimate or test claims about $\theta$.
