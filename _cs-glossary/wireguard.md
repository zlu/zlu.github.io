**WireGuard** is a modern [VPN](/cs-glossary/vpn/) protocol and kernel interface: small codebase, crypto based on Noise, usually over UDP. OpenWrt / iStoreOS expose it as a package for side routers (e.g. NanoPi R5S) when you want a tunnel without putting VPN on the ISP [ONT](/cs-glossary/ont/).
