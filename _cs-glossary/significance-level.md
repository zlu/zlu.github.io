The **significance level** (size) $\alpha$ of a test is its [Type I](/cs-glossary/type-i-error/) error probability: $\alpha=P(\mathrm{reject}\,H_0\mid H_0\ \mathrm{true})$ (or $\sup$ of that probability over a composite null).

Common choices: $0.05$, $0.01$, $0.10$. Compare the [$p$-value](/cs-glossary/p-value/) to $\alpha$, or build the [critical region](/cs-glossary/critical-region/) from the $\alpha$-quantile of the null reference distribution.
