An **arc** is an ordered pair $(i,j)$ in a [digraph](/cs-glossary/directed-graph/) — a one-way link from $i$ to $j$. Flow, cost, and capacity are usually defined on arcs.

[Transportation](/cs-glossary/transportation-problem/), [transshipment](/cs-glossary/transshipment-problem/), and [max-flow](/cs-glossary/maximum-flow/) models route flow along arcs.
