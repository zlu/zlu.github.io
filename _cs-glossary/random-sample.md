A **random sample** of size $n$ is $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ [iid](/cs-glossary/iid/) from a common distribution $f_X$ — mutually independent, identically distributed. Before observation they are random; after observation the numbers $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ are **data**.

A [statistic](/cs-glossary/statistic/) is a function of the sample only. Do not confuse the random sample $X$ with one realised data vector $x$.
