A **discrete random variable** $X$ has a countable range (support) $\mathcal{X}=\{x_1,x_2,\ldots\}$. Probabilities are assigned by a [pmf](/cs-glossary/probability-mass-function/) $f_X(x)=P(X=x)$ with $\sum_{x\in\mathcal{X}}f_X(x)=1$.

Examples: [Bernoulli](/cs-glossary/bernoulli-distribution/), [Binomial](/cs-glossary/binomial-distribution/), [Poisson](/cs-glossary/poisson-distribution/). Contrast [continuous](/cs-glossary/continuous-random-variable/) RVs, which use a density.
