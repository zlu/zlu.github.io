**eMMC** (embedded MultiMediaCard) is soldered flash storage on a board, used like an internal SSD for the OS. Router-style SBCs (e.g. [NanoPi R5S](https://wiki.friendlyelec.com/wiki/index.php/NanoPi_R5S)) often ship with optional eMMC so you can boot [OpenWrt](/cs-glossary/openwrt/) without a microSD card.

Kits are usually sold as board + eMMC size + case + [PSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_supply_unit_(computer)); the eMMC option is what makes a daily-driver flash stickier than a TF-card-only board.
