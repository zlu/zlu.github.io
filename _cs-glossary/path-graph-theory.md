A **path** is a sequence of distinct vertices joined by successive edges/arcs. The **length** of a path is the number of links or the sum of weights.

A [shortest path](/cs-glossary/shortest-path/) minimises that length between two nodes. Closed path with no repeated vertices except start/end ⇒ [cycle](/cs-glossary/cycle-graph-theory/).
