**WGS84** is the global [CRS](/cs-glossary/coordinate-reference-system/) / ellipsoid behind most GPS. Positions as [latitude/longitude](/cs-glossary/latitude-and-longitude/); EPSG code 4326.

Example (Imperial campus ≈): lat $51.498$, lon $-0.177$. For metric network distances, reproject to a local metre-based CRS after ingesting WGS84 traces.
