**Heteroscedasticity** means non-constant error variance across observations or levels of $X$. [OLS](/cs-glossary/ordinary-least-squares/) point estimates can remain [unbiased](/cs-glossary/unbiased-estimator/) under mean exogeneity, but they are no longer [BLUE](/cs-glossary/blue/) and classical SEs / $t$ tests are unreliable.

Remedies: robust (HC) standard errors, weighted least squares, or explicit variance modelling.
