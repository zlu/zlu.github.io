A **statistical summary** compresses a sample into a few numbers — [sample mean](/cs-glossary/sample-mean/), [sample variance](/cs-glossary/sample-variance/), median, quartiles, min/max — each a [statistic](/cs-glossary/statistic/) $T=t(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$.

Summaries describe the sample; [estimators](/cs-glossary/estimator/) use summaries (or other functions) to target population [parameters](/cs-glossary/parameter/).
