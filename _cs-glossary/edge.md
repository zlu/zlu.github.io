An **edge** is an unordered pair $\{u,v\}$ linking two [vertices](/cs-glossary/vertex/) in an [undirected graph](/cs-glossary/undirected-graph/). In transport: a two-way street segment abstracted without orientation.

Contrast directed [arcs](/cs-glossary/arc/). Weighted edges carry length, time, or cost for pathfinding.
