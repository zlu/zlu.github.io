The **$k$th central moment** of $X$ is the [moment](/cs-glossary/moment/) about the mean $\mu=E[X]$:

$$
\mu_k=E[(X-\mu)^k].
$$

$\mu_2=\mathrm{Var}(X)$. Higher central moments feed skewness ($k=3$) and kurtosis ($k=4$). [Standardised moments](/cs-glossary/standardised-moment/) divide by $\sigma^k$.
