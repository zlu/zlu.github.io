A **prior** is the probability (or distribution) assigned to a hypothesis or parameter *before* seeing the current data — $P(E)$ or $p(\theta)$ in a Bayesian update.

[Bayes' theorem](/cs-glossary/bayes-theorem/) multiplies the prior by the [likelihood](/cs-glossary/likelihood/) and renormalises to get the [posterior](/cs-glossary/posterior/). In a diagnostic test, prevalence is the prior for disease.
