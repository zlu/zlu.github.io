An **estimate** is the numerical value of an [estimator](/cs-glossary/estimator/) on observed data: $\hat\theta=w(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$. The estimator $\bar X$ is a random rule; $\bar x=5.2$ is one estimate.

Reporting practice: give the estimate plus uncertainty ([standard error](/cs-glossary/standard-error/) or [confidence interval](/cs-glossary/confidence-interval/)), not a bare number.
