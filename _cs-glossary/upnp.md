**UPnP** (Universal Plug and Play) is a LAN discovery and control family. Devices multicast SSDP advertisements; clients find services without static IPs in the UI.

[DLNA](/cs-glossary/dlna/) media browse rides UPnP AV. Separately, “UPnP IGD” on routers is automatic [NAT](/cs-glossary/nat/) port mapping (a different feature, often disabled for security). Media apps care about the discovery side on a flat [LAN](/cs-glossary/lan/).
