**Spatial autocorrelation** means nearby units have more similar values than distant ones ([Tobler's law](/cs-glossary/toblers-first-law/)). Violates independence ⇒ OLS SEs too small and inference anti-conservative.

Detect with [Moran's I](/cs-glossary/morans-i/); model with [spatial lag](/cs-glossary/spatial-lag-model/) or [spatial error](/cs-glossary/spatial-error-model/) structures using a [spatial weights matrix](/cs-glossary/spatial-weights-matrix/) $W$.
