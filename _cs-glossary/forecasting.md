**Forecasting** predicts future values of a series from past data and/or covariates. Course toolkit: naive/[trend](/cs-glossary/trend/) models, [additive](/cs-glossary/additive-seasonality/)/[multiplicative](/cs-glossary/multiplicative-seasonality/) seasonality, time-series regression, and [ARMA](/cs-glossary/arma/)/[ARIMA](/cs-glossary/arima/).

Good forecasts need diagnostics: residual normality, no leftover [autocorrelation](/cs-glossary/autocorrelation/), and honest out-of-sample checks.
