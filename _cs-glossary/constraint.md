A **constraint** restricts allowed values of the [decision variables](/cs-glossary/decision-variable/) — capacity, demand satisfaction, budget, non-negativity, integrality.

Example: $23x_A+39x_B\ge 220$ (seat capacity), $x_A,x_B\ge 0$, $x_A,x_B\in\mathbb{Z}$. The set of points satisfying all constraints is the [feasible region](/cs-glossary/feasible-region/).
