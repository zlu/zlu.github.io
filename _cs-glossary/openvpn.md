**OpenVPN** is a widely deployed [VPN](/cs-glossary/vpn/) stack (userspace daemon, TLS-based control channel, tun/tap). It is heavier than [WireGuard](/cs-glossary/wireguard/) but still the default in many ISP and commercial VPN profiles. OpenWrt packages both.
