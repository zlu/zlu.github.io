An **experiment** (in probability) is a process with a well-defined set of possible outcomes whose realised outcome is not known in advance. The [sample space](/cs-glossary/sample-space/) $\Omega$ lists those outcomes; an [event](/cs-glossary/event/) is a subset of $\Omega$.

Examples: toss a coin four times; count students in a canteen; measure bulb lifetime in hours. Naming the experiment first fixes whether $\Omega$ is discrete or continuous.
