The **vector** data model represents geography as discrete **points, lines, and polygons** stored as coordinates, preserving topology (connectivity, adjacency).

Formats: `.shp`, `.geojson`, `.kml`, … Ideal for road networks and overlay queries (“how many collisions in this borough?”). Contrast continuous [rasters](/cs-glossary/raster-data-model/).
