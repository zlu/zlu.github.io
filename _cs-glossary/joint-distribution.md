A **joint distribution** specifies probabilities for two or more [random variables](/cs-glossary/random-variable/) together — e.g. $P(X=x,Y=y)$ or a joint [pdf](/cs-glossary/probability-density-function/) $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$.

From the joint you recover [marginals](/cs-glossary/marginal-distribution/) and conditionals. Independence is joint $=$ product of marginals. Covariance and regression relationships are properties of the joint law of $(X,Y)$.
