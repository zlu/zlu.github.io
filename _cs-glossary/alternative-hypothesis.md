The **alternative hypothesis** $H_1$ (or $H_a$) is the claim entertained when evidence against the [null](/cs-glossary/null-hypothesis/) is strong. It may be one-sided ($\theta>\theta_0$) or two-sided ($\theta\neq\theta_0$).

Failing to reject $H_0$ is not “proving $H_0$”; rejecting $H_0$ supports $H_1$ only relative to the model and [Type I](/cs-glossary/type-i-error/) control.
