A **marginal distribution** is the standalone law of one variable obtained from a joint distribution by summing or integrating out the others. For discrete $(X,Y)$, $P(X=x)=\sum_y P(X=x,Y=y)$.

[Conditional](/cs-glossary/conditional-probability/) distributions divide joint by marginal. [Independence](/cs-glossary/independence/) means the joint equals the product of marginals.
