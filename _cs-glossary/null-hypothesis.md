The **null hypothesis** $H_0$ is the default claim under test — often “no effect” ($\beta=0$, $\mu=\mu_0$), i.e. $\theta\in\Omega_0$. Together with the [alternative](/cs-glossary/alternative-hypothesis/) $H_1$ it forms a pair of [complementary hypotheses](/cs-glossary/complementary-hypotheses/).

Tests control [Type I](/cs-glossary/type-i-error/) error under $H_0$. Failure to reject is *not* proof that $H_0$ is true. Report the estimate, SE / CI, and [$p$-value](/cs-glossary/p-value/), not only a reject/accept bit.
