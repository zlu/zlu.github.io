**Longitude** is east–west angle from the Prime Meridian ($-180^\circ$ to $180^\circ$; east positive). **Latitude** is north–south angle from the equator ($-90^\circ$ to $90^\circ$; north positive).

Together they locate a point on the [WGS84](/cs-glossary/wgs84/) ellipsoid. Not a Euclidean plane — use projected CRS for accurate short-range metres.
