An **undirected graph** $G=(V,E)$ has nodes (vertices) $V$ and unordered **edges** $\{u,v\}\in E$. No inherent direction.

Road centreline graphs are sometimes stored undirected; many algorithms ([connected](/cs-glossary/connected-graph/) components, MST) are defined first on undirected graphs.
