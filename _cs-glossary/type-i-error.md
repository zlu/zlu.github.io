A **Type I error** is rejecting $H_0$ when $H_0$ is true (false positive). Its probability is controlled by the test [size](/cs-glossary/significance-level/) $\alpha=P(\mathrm{reject}\mid H_0)$.

Smaller $\alpha$ means fewer false discoveries but typically more [Type II](/cs-glossary/type-ii-error/) errors. The conventional $5\%$ level is a convention, not a law of nature.
