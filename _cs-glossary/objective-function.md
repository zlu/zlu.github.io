The **objective function** is the performance measure to minimise or maximise — weekly operating cost, travel time, unmet demand, etc.

Example: minimise $210 x_A + 360 x_B$ for two bus types. Together with [constraints](/cs-glossary/constraint/) it defines the programme; the optimal value is the best attainable objective over the [feasible region](/cs-glossary/feasible-region/).
