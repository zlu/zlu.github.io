A **GIS** (geographic information system) stores, displays, and analyses spatial data — points, lines, polygons, and rasters — with attributes linked to location.

Transport uses: digital road maps, crash mapping, network analysis, GPS integration / [map matching](/cs-glossary/map-matching/). Core representations: [vector](/cs-glossary/vector-data-model/) and [raster](/cs-glossary/raster-data-model/) models in a [CRS](/cs-glossary/coordinate-reference-system/).
