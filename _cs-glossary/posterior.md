The **posterior** is the updated probability of a hypothesis or parameter after observing data: $P(E\mid F)$ or $p(\theta\mid x)$. By [Bayes' theorem](/cs-glossary/bayes-theorem/),

$$
\text{posterior}\propto \text{likelihood}\times\text{prior}.
$$

In diagnostics, $P(D\mid T)$ is the posterior probability of disease given a positive test — usually much smaller than sensitivity when prevalence is low.
