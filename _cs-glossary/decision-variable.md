A **decision variable** is a quantity the optimiser chooses — e.g. number of type-A buses $x_A$, flow on arc $(i,j)$, or a yes/no assignment.

Types: continuous ($x\ge 0$), [integer](/cs-glossary/integer-programming/) ($x\in\mathbb{Z}$), [binary](/cs-glossary/binary-variable/) ($x\in\{0,1\}$). Linked to a measurable decision in the real system.
