**Seasonality** is a within-year (or within-week) repeating pattern in a [time series](/cs-glossary/time-series/) — quarterly peaks, summer troughs, etc.

Handled by seasonal dummies in regression, [additive](/cs-glossary/additive-seasonality/)/[multiplicative](/cs-glossary/multiplicative-seasonality/) decomposition, or seasonal ARIMA. [ARMA](/cs-glossary/arma/) without seasonal terms assumes no seasonality ([stationarity](/cs-glossary/stationarity/) checklist).
