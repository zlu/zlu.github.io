A **dummy** (indicator) variable equals $1$ when a condition holds and $0$ otherwise — e.g. post-intervention periods, Q2–Q4 seasonal dummies, or a treatment group.

In $Y=\alpha+\beta D+\varepsilon$, $\beta$ is the mean difference between $D=1$ and $D=0$ groups (given the rest of the model). Essential for [intervention](/cs-glossary/intervention-model/) and seasonality coding.
