**Tobler's first law of geography** (1970): “Everything is related to everything else, but near things are more related than distant things.”

The verbal justification for [spatial autocorrelation](/cs-glossary/spatial-autocorrelation/) and distance-decay [weights](/cs-glossary/spatial-weights-matrix/) in spatial models of crashes, prices, and weather.
