A **metaheuristic** is a high-level heuristic strategy for hard combinatorial problems when exact [IP](/cs-glossary/integer-programming/) is too slow — local search, simulated annealing, tabu search, [genetic algorithms](/cs-glossary/genetic-algorithm-optimisation/).

No optimality guarantee; aims for good feasible solutions via intelligently exploring the search space. Course bridge from exact network LPs to approximate methods.
