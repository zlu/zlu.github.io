**Statistical inference** draws conclusions about a population or parameter $\theta$ from data modelled as realisations of [random variables](/cs-glossary/random-variable/). Typical parametric family: $\{f(x;\theta):\theta\in\Omega_\theta\}$.

Three aims: **[point estimation](/cs-glossary/point-estimation/)**, **[hypothesis testing](/cs-glossary/hypothesis-testing/)**, and **[interval estimation](/cs-glossary/confidence-interval/)**. Tools include [MoM](/cs-glossary/method-of-moments/), [MLE](/cs-glossary/maximum-likelihood/), [bias](/cs-glossary/bias)/[MSE](/cs-glossary/mean-squared-error/), [LLN](/cs-glossary/law-of-large-numbers/), and the [CLT](/cs-glossary/central-limit-theorem/).
