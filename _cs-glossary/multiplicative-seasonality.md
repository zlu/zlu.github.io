A **multiplicative** seasonal forecast is

$$
\widehat Y = \mathrm{Trend}\times\mathrm{Seasonal\ index}\times\mathrm{Cyclical}\times\mathrm{Error}.
$$

The [seasonal index](/cs-glossary/seasonal-index/) is a unit-free factor (average of season/annual-mean ratios). Prefer multiplicative when seasonal amplitude scales with the level of the series.
