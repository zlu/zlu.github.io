The **Gauss–Markov theorem** says that under linearity in parameters, [exogeneity](/cs-glossary/exogeneity/)/zero conditional mean errors, [homoscedasticity](/cs-glossary/homoscedasticity/), and uncorrelated errors, the [OLS](/cs-glossary/ordinary-least-squares/) estimator is **[BLUE](/cs-glossary/blue/)**: best linear unbiased estimator — minimum variance in the linear unbiased class.

Violations: endogeneity ⇒ bias; hetero or correlated errors ⇒ OLS stays unbiased (under mean independence) but loses efficiency and usual SEs mislead.
