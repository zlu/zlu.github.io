$X_n\xrightarrow{p}X$ (**convergence in probability**) means: for every $\varepsilon>0$, $P(\lvert X_n-X\rvert<\varepsilon)\to 1$ as $n\to\infty$. [Consistency](/cs-glossary/consistency/) of estimators is $W_n\xrightarrow{p}\theta$.

The [weak LLN](/cs-glossary/law-of-large-numbers/) is $\bar X_n\xrightarrow{p}\mu$. Stronger modes (a.s. convergence) exist but course work usually uses $\xrightarrow{p}$ and $\xrightarrow{d}$.
