**[iStoreOS](https://site.istoreos.com/en/)** is an [OpenWrt](/cs-glossary/openwrt/)-based appliance OS with a China-oriented UI and an **iStore** app catalog (one-click-ish installs for miniDLNA, [VPN](/cs-glossary/vpn/), etc.). Source: [github.com/istoreos/istoreos](https://github.com/istoreos/istoreos).

Useful when you want a [bypass router](/cs-glossary/bypass-router/) / light [NAS](/cs-glossary/nas/) without living in raw LuCI. You can still re-flash plain OpenWrt or [ImmortalWrt](/cs-glossary/immortalwrt/) later.
