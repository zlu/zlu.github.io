The **dependent variable** (response / outcome), denoted $Y$, is the random variable whose variation a [regression](/cs-glossary/linear-regression/) seeks to explain conditional on observables. In $Y=E[Y\mid X]+\varepsilon$, the left-hand side is the dependent variable; the [regression function](/cs-glossary/regression-function/) $\mu(X,\beta)=E[Y\mid X]$ describes how its mean moves with the [covariates](/cs-glossary/independent-variable/).
