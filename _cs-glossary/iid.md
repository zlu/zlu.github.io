**IID** means independent and identically distributed: each $X_i$ has the same distribution and the variables are mutually [independent](/cs-glossary/independence/). Standard assumption for a [random sample](/cs-glossary/random-sample/).

IID unlocks product [likelihoods](/cs-glossary/likelihood/), the [LLN](/cs-glossary/law-of-large-numbers/), and the [CLT](/cs-glossary/central-limit-theorem/). Dependence (e.g. time-series serial correlation) breaks these plug-and-play results.
