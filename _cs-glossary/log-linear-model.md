A **log-linear** (log-lin) model is

$$
\log Y_i=\alpha+\beta X_i+\varepsilon_i.
$$

A one-unit rise in $X$ multiplies expected $Y$ by about $e^\beta$ ($\approx 1+\beta$ for small $\beta$), i.e. a $100\beta\%$ change. Linearises $Y=\alpha_0 e^{\beta X}$. Contrast [lin-log](/cs-glossary/lin-log-model/) and [log-log](/cs-glossary/log-log-model/).
