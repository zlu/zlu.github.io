The **transshipment problem** extends transportation by allowing **intermediate** nodes that neither supply nor demand net product — flow can pass through.

Find arc flows minimising cost while respecting supplies, demands, and flow balance at transshipment nodes. Special case of [minimum-cost flow](/cs-glossary/minimum-cost-flow/).
