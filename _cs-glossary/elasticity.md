**Elasticity** is a unit-free measure of responsiveness: the percent change in $Y$ for a 1% change in $X$. In a [log-log](/cs-glossary/log-log-model/) regression $\log Y=\alpha+\beta\log X+\varepsilon$, the slope $\beta$ *is* the elasticity.

In levels $Y=\alpha+\beta X+\varepsilon$, elasticity at a point is $\beta\cdot(\bar X/\bar Y)$ (or $X/Y$ at a chosen point). Useful when comparing effects across differently scaled covariates.
