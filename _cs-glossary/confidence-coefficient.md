The **confidence coefficient** of an interval estimator is

$$
\inf_\theta P_\theta\bigl(\theta\in[g_L(X),g_U(X)]\bigr)=1-\alpha.
$$

We then call $[g_L,g_U]$ a $100(1-\alpha)\%$ [confidence interval](/cs-glossary/confidence-interval/). Linked to tests: inverting a level-$\alpha$ test yields coefficient $1-\alpha$.
