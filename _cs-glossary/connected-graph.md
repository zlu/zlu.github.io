An [undirected](/cs-glossary/undirected-graph/) graph is **connected** if every pair of vertices is joined by some [path](/cs-glossary/path-graph-theory/). Maximal connected pieces are connected components.

Networks used for routing must be connected (or analysed component-wise). Strong connectivity is the directed analogue (path $u\to v$ and $v\to u$).
