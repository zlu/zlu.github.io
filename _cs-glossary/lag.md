A **lag** of order $k$ is the value of a series $k$ periods earlier: $Y_{t-k}$. Lagged regressors $Y_{t-1}$, $X_{t-1}$ appear in autoregressions and distributed-lag models.

[ACF](/cs-glossary/autocorrelation-function/)/[PACF](/cs-glossary/partial-autocorrelation/) are functions of the lag index $k=0,1,2,\ldots$.
