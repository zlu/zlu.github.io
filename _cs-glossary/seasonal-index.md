A **seasonal index** for season $s$ is the average ratio of that season’s value to the corresponding annual average — the multiplicative seasonal factor.

Example: quarterly indices $0.61,0.63,1.20,1.56$ mean Q4 runs ~56% above the annual average. Forecast: $\widehat Y_{t}=\widehat{\mathrm{Trend}}_t\times\mathrm{Index}_{s(t)}$.
