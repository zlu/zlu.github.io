**[ImmortalWrt](https://github.com/immortalwrt/immortalwrt)** is an [OpenWrt](/cs-glossary/openwrt/) fork aimed at mainland China users: extra packages, device profiles, and localization defaults. Many community board images (including some NanoPi R5S builds) are ImmortalWrt-based. Downloads: [downloads.immortalwrt.org](https://downloads.immortalwrt.org/).
