**Linearity of expectation** says $E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y]$ for constants $a,b$, with no independence requirement. Extends to any finite sum: $E[\sum_i X_i]=\sum_i E[X_i]$.

This is why indicators make counting arguments easy and why $E[\bar X]=\mu$ for any [iid](/cs-glossary/iid/) sample with mean $\mu$ — a building block for [unbiased](/cs-glossary/unbiased-estimator/) estimation.
