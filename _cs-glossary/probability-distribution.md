A **probability distribution** of a [random variable](/cs-glossary/random-variable/) $X$ is the rule $f_X$ that assigns probability to possible values — a [pmf](/cs-glossary/probability-mass-function/) if discrete, a [pdf](/cs-glossary/probability-density-function/) if continuous. Equivalently specified by the [CDF](/cs-glossary/cumulative-distribution-function/) $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$.

The **support** is the set of $x$ with positive probability / density. Parametric families (Normal, Poisson, …) index distributions by $\theta\in\Omega_\theta$.
