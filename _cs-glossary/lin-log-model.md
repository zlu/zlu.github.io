A **lin-log** model keeps $Y$ in levels and logs the regressor:

$$
Y_i=\alpha+\beta\log X_i+\varepsilon_i.
$$

A 1% rise in $X$ changes expected $Y$ by about $\beta/100$ units. Linearises forms like $Y=\alpha+\beta\log X$. Elasticity at a point is $\beta/\bar Y$ (approx).
