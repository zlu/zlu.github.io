**Disjoint** (mutually exclusive) events $E$ and $F$ satisfy $E\cap F=\emptyset$: they cannot occur together. A collection $E_1,E_2,\ldots$ is **pairwise disjoint** if $E_i\cap E_j=\emptyset$ for all $i\neq j$.

For pairwise disjoint events the probability [axioms](/cs-glossary/probability-axioms/) give countable additivity: $P(\bigcup_i E_i)=\sum_i P(E_i)$. Used to build partitions for the [law of total probability](/cs-glossary/law-of-total-probability/).
