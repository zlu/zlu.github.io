An **additive** seasonal forecast is

$$
\widehat Y = \mathrm{Trend}+\mathrm{Seasonal\ component}+\mathrm{Cyclical}+\mathrm{Error}.
$$

Seasonal components are average deviations of each season from the annual mean (same units as $Y$). Prefer additive when seasonal swings stay roughly constant in level as the series grows.
