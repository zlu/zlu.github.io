A **directed graph** (digraph) $G=(V,A)$ has nodes $V$ and **arcs** $A$ as ordered pairs $(i,j)$. Direction matters: $(i,j)\neq(j,i)$ in general. A loop (buckle) connects a node to itself.

Transport networks, web links, and flow models are naturally directed. [Shortest paths](/cs-glossary/shortest-path/) and max-flow use digraphs.
