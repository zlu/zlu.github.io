The **Exponential**($\lambda$) distribution (rate parametrisation) has pdf $f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$ for $x>0$. Mean $1/\lambda$, variance $1/\lambda^2$. Memoryless: remaining lifetime is independent of age.

It models waiting times and is the continuous counterpart to Poisson process inter-arrival times. Support $(0,\infty)$.
