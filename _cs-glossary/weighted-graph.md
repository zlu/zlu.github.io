A **weighted graph** assigns a numeric weight (length, time, cost, capacity) to each edge/arc. Pathfinding minimises (or maximises) the sum of weights along a [path](/cs-glossary/path-graph-theory/).

Input to Dijkstra/[A*](/algos/astar/), [min-cost flow](/cs-glossary/minimum-cost-flow/), and assignment costs $c_{ij}$.
