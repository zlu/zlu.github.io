**Cross-sectional data** are observations on many units (people, firms, regions) at the *same* point in time. Classic [OLS](/cs-glossary/ordinary-least-squares/) teaching assumes roughly [iid](/cs-glossary/iid/) rows.

Contrast [time series](/cs-glossary/time-series/) (one unit over time) and [panel](/cs-glossary/panel-data/) (many units over time). Applying plain cross-section OLS to time series often ignores [serial correlation](/cs-glossary/autocorrelation/).
