**Order statistics** are the sorted sample values $X_{(1)}\le\cdots\le X_{(n)}$. The sample minimum is $X_{(1)}$, maximum $X_{(n)}$, and the sample [median](/cs-glossary/median/) uses the middle order statistic(s).

Quartiles and the [IQR](/cs-glossary/interquartile-range/) are functions of order statistics. They underpin robust summaries and many nonparametric procedures.
