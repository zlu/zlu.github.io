A **statistic** $T=t(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ is a random variable that depends on the [random sample](/cs-glossary/random-sample/) only — not on unknown parameters except through the $X_i$. Examples: [sample mean](/cs-glossary/sample-mean/) $\bar X$, [sample variance](/cs-glossary/sample-variance/) $S^2$, order statistics.

The law of $T$ over repeated samples is its [sampling distribution](/cs-glossary/sampling-distribution/). An [estimator](/cs-glossary/estimator/) is a statistic used to estimate a parameter.
