**Positive serial correlation** means a residual tends to be followed by one of the same sign. [Durbin–Watson](/cs-glossary/durbin-watson/) $d$ falls substantially below $2$ (especially $<1$).

Common in slowly moving transport series (demand, accidents). Inflates $t$-statistics if ignored.
