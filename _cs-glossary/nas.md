A **NAS** (network attached storage) is a device built to share disks on a [LAN](/cs-glossary/lan/): real filesystem drivers ([ExFAT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/exFAT), ext4, etc.), [Samba](/cs-glossary/samba/) / NFS, often [DLNA](/cs-glossary/dlna/), and enough USB / SATA power for disks.

An ISP [ONT](/cs-glossary/ont/) with USB ports is not a NAS. It may expose a weak share UI and only [FAT32](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File_Allocation_Table#FAT32)-class volumes.
