**Negative serial correlation** means a positive residual is likely followed by a negative one (and vice versa). [Durbin–Watson](/cs-glossary/durbin-watson/) $d$ rises substantially above $2$ (toward $4$).

Less common than positive serial correlation in levels data; can appear after over-differencing.
