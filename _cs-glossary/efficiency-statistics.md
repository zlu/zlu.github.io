**Efficiency** compares [estimators](/cs-glossary/estimator/): $W_1$ is more efficient than $W_2$ if it has smaller variance (often among unbiased estimators). Relative efficiency is a variance ratio.

The **UMVUE** (uniformly minimum-variance unbiased estimator) is best in the unbiased class when it exists. [Gauss–Markov](/cs-glossary/gauss-markov-theorem/) is an efficiency result for linear estimators of regression coefficients ([BLUE](/cs-glossary/blue/)).
