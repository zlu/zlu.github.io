The **median** is a 0.5-[quantile](/cs-glossary/quantile/): a value $m$ with at least half the mass on each side. For a sorted sample, the middle order statistic (or average of two middles if $n$ even).

More robust to outliers than the [sample mean](/cs-glossary/sample-mean/). For symmetric distributions (e.g. Normal), mean $=$ median in the population.
