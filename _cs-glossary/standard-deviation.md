**Standard deviation** $\sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}$ is spread on the same scale as $X$. For $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, about 68% / 95% of mass lies within $1\sigma$ / $2\sigma$ of $\mu$ (exact Normal rules of thumb).

The sample SD $S=\sqrt{S^2}$ uses the unbiased [sample variance](/cs-glossary/sample-variance/). In regression, residual SD $s=\sqrt{\mathrm{RSS}/(n-2)}$ feeds [standard errors](/cs-glossary/standard-error/).
