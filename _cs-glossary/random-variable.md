A **random variable** (RV) is a real-valued function $X:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}$ of the outcome of a random [experiment](/cs-glossary/experiment/). Discrete RVs have a [pmf](/cs-glossary/probability-mass-function/); continuous RVs have a [pdf](/cs-glossary/probability-density-function/) and [cdf](/cs-glossary/cumulative-distribution-function/) $F(x)=P(X\le x)$.

Named families used early in inference: [Normal](/cs-glossary/normal-distribution/), [Bernoulli](/cs-glossary/bernoulli-distribution/), [Poisson](/cs-glossary/poisson-distribution/), [Exponential](/cs-glossary/exponential-distribution/). [Expectation](/cs-glossary/expectation/) and [variance](/cs-glossary/variance/) summarise location and spread.
