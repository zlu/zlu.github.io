**LuCI** is the standard web UI for [OpenWrt](/cs-glossary/openwrt/) (and most forks): menus for network, firewall, and `opkg` software packages. [iStoreOS](/cs-glossary/istoreos/) wraps or replaces a lot of that flow with a more appliance-like Chinese UI and app store.
