The **coverage probability** of an interval procedure $[g_L(X),g_U(X)]$ at $\theta$ is

$$
P_\theta\bigl(\theta\in[g_L(X),g_U(X)]\bigr).
$$

The [confidence coefficient](/cs-glossary/confidence-coefficient/) is the infimum of coverage over $\theta$. A 95% CI means the *procedure* covers the truth in 95% of repeated samples — not that $P(\theta\in\mathrm{this\ interval}\mid\mathrm{data})=0.95$ in a Bayesian sense.
