A **binary tree** is a tree where each node has at most two children (left and right). It is a special [graph](/cs-glossary/graph/): connected, acyclic, rooted for most algorithm discussions.

Height can be $n-1$ when skewed or $\Theta(\log n)$ when balanced. [Heaps](/cs-glossary/heap/) and [BSTs](/cs-glossary/binary-search-tree/) add order constraints on top of the binary shape.

Traversals: preorder, inorder, postorder ([DFS](/cs-glossary/dfs/) styles), and level-order ([BFS](/cs-glossary/bfs/)). See [tree traversal](/cs-glossary/tree-traversal/).
