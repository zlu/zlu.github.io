**Panel data** record multiple entities each observed at several times — a blend of [cross-section](/cs-glossary/cross-sectional-data/) and [time series](/cs-glossary/time-series/).

Dependence can run within entity over time (serial) and across entities in space ([spatial autocorrelation](/cs-glossary/spatial-autocorrelation/)). Modelling options include entity fixed effects, clustered SEs, and dynamic panels.
