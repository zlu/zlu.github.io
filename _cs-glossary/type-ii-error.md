A **Type II error** is failing to reject $H_0$ when $H_1$ is true (false negative). If $\beta(\theta)=P(\mathrm{accept}\mid\theta)$ for $\theta$ in the alternative, then [power](/cs-glossary/power-of-a-test/) is $1-\beta(\theta)$.

Power rises with larger effects, larger $n$, and larger $\alpha$. Reporting only “insignificant” without power context can hide underpowered studies.
