A **probability mass function** (pmf) $f_X(x)=P(X=x)$ specifies a discrete [random variable](/cs-glossary/random-variable/). Validity: $f(x)\ge 0$ for all $x$ and $\sum_x f(x)=1$.

On a finite support you often solve for a normalising constant $c$ so the pmf sums to one. Contrast with a [pdf](/cs-glossary/probability-density-function/), where point masses are zero and probabilities come from integrals.
