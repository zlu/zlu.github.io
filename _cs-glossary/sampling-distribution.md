The **sampling distribution** of a [statistic](/cs-glossary/statistic/) $T$ is the probability distribution of $T$ induced by repeatedly drawing [random samples](/cs-glossary/random-sample/) and recomputing $t$. Mental picture: histogram of many simulated $\hat\theta$ values.

[Bias](/cs-glossary/bias/), [variance](/cs-glossary/variance/), and [MSE](/cs-glossary/mean-squared-error/) are properties of this distribution. The [CLT](/cs-glossary/central-limit-theorem/) approximates the sampling distribution of $\bar X$ by a Normal.
