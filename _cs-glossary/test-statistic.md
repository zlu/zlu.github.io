A **test statistic** $T(X)$ is a function of the sample used to decide a hypothesis test. Under $H_0$, $T$ has a known (or approximate) reference distribution; extreme observed $t_{\mathrm{obs}}$ yield small [$p$-values](/cs-glossary/p-value/).

Examples: $z$-score, [$t$](/cs-glossary/t-test/), [$F$](/cs-glossary/f-test/), Durbin–Watson $d$. The [critical region](/cs-glossary/critical-region/) is the set of $T$ values that reject $H_0$.
