The **law of large numbers** (LLN) says that for iid $X_i$ with mean $\mu$, the [sample mean](/cs-glossary/sample-mean/) converges to $\mu$: $\bar X_n\xrightarrow{p}\mu$ (weak LLN). Averages settle; single draws do not.

LLN underwrites [consistency](/cs-glossary/consistency/) of $\bar X$ and many MoM/MLE plug-ins. It does not give rates or Normal shape — that is the [CLT](/cs-glossary/central-limit-theorem/).
