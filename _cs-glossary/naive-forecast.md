A **naive** (simple) forecast uses only the history of the target — typically a [trend](/cs-glossary/trend/) line $Y_t=b_0+b_1 t+u_t$ with no other covariates and no seasonal adjustment.

Baseline to beat with seasonality, regression, or ARIMA. Useful pedagogy; rarely the final transport-intervention model.
