$X_n\xrightarrow{d}X$ (**convergence in distribution**, in law) means the [cdfs](/cs-glossary/cumulative-distribution-function/) of $X_n$ converge to the cdf of $X$ at continuity points of the limit.

The [CLT](/cs-glossary/central-limit-theorem/) is the headline example: standardised sample means $\xrightarrow{d}N(0,1)$. Convergence in probability implies convergence in distribution; the converse needs a degenerate (constant) limit.
