A **$p$-value** is the probability, computed under $H_0$, of a test statistic at least as extreme as the one observed. Small $p$ (e.g. $<0.05$) ⇒ data unusual under the [null](/cs-glossary/null-hypothesis/) ⇒ evidence against $H_0$.

It is not the probability that $H_0$ is true, nor the posterior error rate. Match one- vs two-sided definitions to the [$t$/$z$](/cs-glossary/t-test/) procedure you use.
