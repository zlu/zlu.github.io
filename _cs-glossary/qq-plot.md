A **Q–Q plot** graphs sample [quantiles](/cs-glossary/quantile/) against theoretical quantiles (usually [Normal](/cs-glossary/normal-distribution/)). Residuals on a straight line ⇒ distributional assumption looks plausible; systematic curves/tails ⇒ skewness or heavy tails.

Used with histogram and [K–S](/cs-glossary/kolmogorov-smirnov-test/) tests when checking Normal errors before trusting exact $t$/$F$ inference.
