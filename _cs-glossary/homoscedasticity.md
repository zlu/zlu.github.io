**Homoscedasticity** means constant error variance: $\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon_i)=\sigma^2$ for all $i$ (often also $\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon\mid X)=\sigma^2$). It is one of the [Gauss–Markov](/cs-glossary/gauss-markov-theorem/) conditions for OLS to be [BLUE](/cs-glossary/blue/).

Opposite: [heteroscedasticity](/cs-glossary/heteroscedasticity/). With hetero errors, usual [standard errors](/cs-glossary/standard-error/) are wrong; use robust SEs or a different estimator.
