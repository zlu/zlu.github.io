A **Binomial**($n,p$) count is the number of successes in $n$ independent [Bernoulli](/cs-glossary/bernoulli-distribution/)($p$) trials. [PMF](/cs-glossary/probability-mass-function/): $P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$. Mean $np$, variance $np(1-p)$.

Related limits: Poisson approximation for rare events; Normal approximation for large $n$ ([CLT](/cs-glossary/central-limit-theorem/)).
