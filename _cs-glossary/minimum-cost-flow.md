**Minimum-cost flow** sends flow through a capacitated network to satisfy supplies/demands at minimum total arc cost. Balance constraints at each node; capacity bounds on arcs.

Unifies [transportation](/cs-glossary/transportation-problem/), [transshipment](/cs-glossary/transshipment-problem/), and many routing LPs. Efficient combinatorial algorithms exploit network structure beyond general LP.
