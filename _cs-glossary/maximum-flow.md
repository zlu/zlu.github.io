The **maximum-flow** problem asks for the largest feasible flow from a source $s$ to a sink $t$ through capacitated [arcs](/cs-glossary/arc/), ignoring costs.

Max-flow / min-cut theorem: max $s$–$t$ flow equals the minimum capacity of an $s$–$t$ cut. Building block for circulation and some matching algorithms.
