The **support** of a [random variable](/cs-glossary/random-variable/) / distribution is the set of values that can occur with positive probability (discrete) or where the density is positive (continuous). Written $\mathcal{X}$ or $\mathrm{supp}(f_X)$ in the notes.

Knowing the support constrains legitimate estimators and likelihoods (e.g. Uniform$(0,\theta)$ support depends on $\theta$).
