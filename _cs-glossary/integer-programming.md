**Integer programming** (IP/ILP) is LP plus integrality: some or all variables $\in\mathbb{Z}$. **MIP** mixes continuous and integer vars.

Models countable decisions (vehicles, crews) and yes/no choices via [binary](/cs-glossary/binary-variable/) variables. Harder than LP (combinatorial); solved by branch-and-bound / cutting planes. Network [assignment](/cs-glossary/assignment-problem/) is a structured IP with total unimodularity shortcuts.
