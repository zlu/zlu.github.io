**Power** of a test at parameter $\theta$ is $1-\beta(\theta)=P(\mathrm{reject}\,H_0\mid\theta)$ for $\theta$ in the alternative — the chance of detecting a real effect.

Power increases with $|\theta-\theta_0|$, sample size $n$, and [significance level](/cs-glossary/significance-level/) $\alpha$, and decreases with noise $\sigma$. Underpowered tests often produce large [$p$-values](/cs-glossary/p-value/) even when $H_1$ is true.
