A **genetic algorithm** evolves a population of candidate solutions with selection, crossover, and mutation — a population-based [metaheuristic](/cs-glossary/metaheuristic/).

Used in the course alongside pathfinding/clustering for network design and combinatorial transport problems where exact MIP does not scale. (See also the dedicated [algos write-up](/algos/genetic-algorithm/).)
