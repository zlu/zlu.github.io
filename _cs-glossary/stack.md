A **stack** is a LIFO (last-in, first-out) collection: `push`, `pop`, and usually `peek` on one end. Implement with an [array](/cs-glossary/array/) or [linked list](/cs-glossary/linked-list/).

Call stacks for [recursion](/cs-glossary/recursion/), undo buffers, and the explicit stack in iterative [DFS](/cs-glossary/dfs/) are the usual appearances. Parenthesis matching and expression evaluation are standard exercises.

All core operations are $O(1)$ time for a well-implemented stack.
