In a testing problem the **complementary hypotheses** are the [null](/cs-glossary/null-hypothesis/) $H_0:\theta\in\Omega_0$ and [alternative](/cs-glossary/alternative-hypothesis/) $H_1:\theta\in\Omega_0^c$ — a partition of the parameter space.

Assume $H_0$, derive a [test statistic](/cs-glossary/test-statistic/) with known null sampling distribution, and reject when the data look too extreme under $H_0$.
