The **feasible region** is the set of all [decision-variable](/cs-glossary/decision-variable/) vectors that satisfy every [constraint](/cs-glossary/constraint/).

In LP it is a convex polyhedron; optima occur at extreme points (vertices) — the geometric fact behind the [simplex](/cs-glossary/simplex-method/) method. Empty feasible region ⇒ infeasible model; unbounded region with improving ray ⇒ unbounded problem.
