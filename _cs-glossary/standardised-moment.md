The **$k$th standardised moment** scales the [central moment](/cs-glossary/central-moment/) by $\sigma^k$:

$$
E\!\left[\left(\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^k\right]=\frac{\mu_k}{\sigma^k}.
$$

For $k=3$ this is skewness; for $k=4$, kurtosis (conventions vary on subtracting 3). Unit-free shape summaries of a distribution.
