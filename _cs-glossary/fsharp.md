**F#** is a functional-first language on [.NET](/cs-glossary/dotnet/). Shares the CLR with [C#](/cs-glossary/csharp/); favors immutability, algebraic data types, and concise scripts / services.

```fsharp
let greet name = $"hello, {name}"

printfn "%s" (greet "world")
```
