A **vertex** (node) is an element of the vertex set $V$ of a [graph](/cs-glossary/graph/) — an intersection, stop, depot, or zone centroid in transport networks.

Edges/arcs connect pairs of vertices. Degree = number of incident edges (in/out-degree if directed).
