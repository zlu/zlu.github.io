---
title: Remux
synonyms:
  - Remultiplex
  - Remuxed
---
A **remux** (remultiplex) copies existing video, audio, and subtitle streams into a new [container](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_container_format) (often Matroska `.mkv`) **without re-encoding**. Bitrate and quality stay the same as the source; only the wrapper changes.

Blu-ray and UHD remuxes keep the disc’s original streams, so files commonly run **tens of GB** and often exceed FAT32’s **4GB** per-file limit. A re-encode (transcode) would shrink the file by compressing again; a remux does not.
