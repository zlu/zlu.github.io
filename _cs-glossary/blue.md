**BLUE** means **best linear unbiased estimator**: among estimators that are linear in the outcomes and [unbiased](/cs-glossary/unbiased-estimator/), it has the smallest variance. [Gauss–Markov](/cs-glossary/gauss-markov-theorem/) identifies [OLS](/cs-glossary/ordinary-least-squares/) as BLUE under classical linear-model assumptions.

“Best” is variance efficiency inside a restricted class — not automatically minimum [MSE](/cs-glossary/mean-squared-error/) among all estimators.
