The **interquartile range** (IQR) is $Q_3-Q_1$, the spread of the middle 50% of a distribution or sample. It is a robust scale measure: less sensitive to extremes than the range or SD.

Often paired with the [median](/cs-glossary/median/) in descriptive summaries and boxplots. Computed from [order statistics](/cs-glossary/order-statistic/) / sample [quantiles](/cs-glossary/quantile/).
