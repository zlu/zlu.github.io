**HFS+** (Mac OS Extended, often Journaled) is the older macOS volume format, still common on aging external disks. Like [APFS](/cs-glossary/apfs/), it is not useful on typical router/ONT USB shares. Prefer [ExFAT](/cs-glossary/exfat/) or [FAT32](/cs-glossary/fat32/) for cross-device sticks.
