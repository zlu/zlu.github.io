The **probability function** $P(\cdot)$ assigns weight to [events](/cs-glossary/event/) (sets of outcomes). Kolmogorov [axioms](/cs-glossary/probability-axioms/): non-negativity, $P(\Omega)=1$, and countable additivity over [disjoint](/cs-glossary/disjoint-events/) unions.

All of [conditional probability](/cs-glossary/conditional-probability/), Bayes, and expectation sit on top of $P$.
