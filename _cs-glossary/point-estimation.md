**Point estimation** produces a single-number guess $\hat\theta$ for an unknown parameter. Core methods: [method of moments](/cs-glossary/method-of-moments/) and [maximum likelihood](/cs-glossary/maximum-likelihood/).

Judge estimators with [bias](/cs-glossary/bias/), variance, [MSE](/cs-glossary/mean-squared-error/), and [consistency](/cs-glossary/consistency/). Contrast with [interval estimation](/cs-glossary/confidence-interval/), which reports a range.
