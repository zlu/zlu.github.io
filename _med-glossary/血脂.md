**中文：** 血中脂类总称，包括胆固醇、甘油三酯等。必需但过高（尤其 LDL 或甘油三酯）会增加动脉粥样硬化与心脑血管病风险。

**English:** Umbrella term for blood fats—cholesterol, triglycerides, and related particles. Excess LDL or triglycerides raises atherosclerotic risk.
