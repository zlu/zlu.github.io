**中文：** 角膜上皮出现散在微小点状缺损，多与干眼、长时间用眼、配戴接触镜等有关。荧光素染色可见点状着染；症状可为干涩、畏光或短暂刺痛。

**English:** Scattered pinpoint defects of the corneal epithelium, often from dry eye, screen use, or contact lenses. Fluorescein shows punctate staining; symptoms include grit, photophobia, or brief stabs.
