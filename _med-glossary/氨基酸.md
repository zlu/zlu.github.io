**中文：** 蛋白质的基本组成单位。必需氨基酸需从食物获取；非必需氨基酸人体可自行合成。对肌肉、酶与激素功能重要。

**English:** Building blocks of protein. Essential amino acids must come from food; nonessential ones the body can make. Key for muscle, enzymes, and hormones.
