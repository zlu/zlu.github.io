**中文：** 上腹部器官：外分泌胰液帮助消化；内分泌胰岛分泌胰岛素、胰高血糖素等调节血糖。相关疾病包括胰腺炎、胰腺癌与糖尿病。

**English:** Gland with exocrine digestive enzymes and endocrine hormones (insulin, glucagon) that regulate blood sugar. Disease links include pancreatitis and diabetes.
