**中文：** 一类不饱和脂肪酸（如 EPA、DHA、ALA），主要来自深海鱼、亚麻籽等。常与心血管健康、抗炎及大脑功能相关。

**English:** Unsaturated fats (EPA, DHA, ALA) found in fatty fish and flaxseed; linked to cardiovascular and anti-inflammatory benefits.
