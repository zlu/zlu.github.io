**中文：** 血液中的葡萄糖，是主要能量来源，受胰岛素等调节。空腹大致约 3.9–6.1 mmol/L；持续过高与糖尿病相关，过低可致心慌、意识改变。

**English:** Glucose in blood—the body’s main ready fuel, regulated by insulin and other hormones. Chronically high levels link to diabetes; lows can be dangerous.
