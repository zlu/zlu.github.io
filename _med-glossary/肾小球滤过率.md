**中文：** 单位时间内肾脏生成超滤液的量，反映清除代谢废物的能力。成人大致约 80–120 mL/min，随年龄下降；临床多用血肌酐估算的 eGFR。

**English:** How much filtrate the kidneys make per minute—the core measure of filtering ability. Often reported as creatinine-based eGFR; declines with age and kidney disease.
