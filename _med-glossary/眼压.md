**中文：** 眼球内部液体形成的压力。眼压明显升高可见于青光眼发作；急性闭角型发作的剧痛通常持续更久，并伴视力改变等，极少表现为仅 1 秒的刺痛。

**English:** Pressure inside the eyeball. Marked elevation occurs in glaucoma attacks; acute angle-closure pain lasts minutes or longer with visual symptoms—not a one-second jab—but IOP is still measured to rule it out.
