**中文：** 肌肉代谢产生的废物，主要由肾脏滤过排出。血肌酐升高常提示肾小球滤过功能下降；解读需结合肌肉量、eGFR 与尿蛋白等。

**English:** **Creatinine** (Chinese: 肌酐) is muscle-breakdown waste filtered by the kidneys. Rising blood creatinine often means lower filtration; interpret with muscle mass, eGFR, and urine protein.
