**中文：** 植物性食物中人体难消化的成分。可溶性纤维有助于调节血糖与胆固醇；不可溶性纤维促进肠道蠕动。来自全谷、豆类、蔬果等。

**English:** Indigestible plant carbs. Soluble fiber helps glucose/cholesterol; insoluble fiber aids bowel transit. Found in whole grains, legumes, fruits, and vegetables.
