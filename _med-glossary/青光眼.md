**中文：** 以视神经损害和特征性视野缺损为特征的一组眼病，多数与病理性高眼压相关。慢性型可隐匿进展；急性发作则疼痛剧烈且持续时间较长，并常伴视力变化。

**English:** Optic-nerve damage with characteristic visual-field loss, often linked to elevated IOP. Chronic forms can progress quietly; acute attacks bring lasting pain plus vision changes, not fleeting stabs alone.
