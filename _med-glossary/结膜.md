**中文：** 覆盖巩膜表面与眼睑内侧的薄层黏膜。结膜充血表现为“红眼”；上睑结膜囊可藏睫毛或微小异物。

**English:** Thin mucosa covering the white of the eye and inner lids. Injection causes a red eye; the upper fornix can hide a lash or particle.
