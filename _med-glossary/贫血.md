**中文：** 血红蛋白或红细胞携氧能力下降，引起乏力、苍白等；需查明缺铁、慢性病等病因。

**English:** Reduced oxygen-carrying capacity of blood—fatigue, pallor; causes include iron deficiency and chronic disease.
