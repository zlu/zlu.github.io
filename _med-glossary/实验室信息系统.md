**中文：** 检验科业务系统（LIS）。负责标本流转、结果审核与报告单打印。双栏血常规等版式往往来自 LIS 打印模板，而不是检查项目本身。

**English:** Laboratory information system (LIS). Hospital software for orders, specimens, result release, and printed reports. Dual-panel CBC layouts usually come from the LIS print template.
