**中文：** 嘌呤代谢的终产物，主要由肾脏排出。血尿酸升高（高尿酸血症）可增加痛风风险，也可能提示排泄减少或代谢紊乱。

**English:** End product of purine metabolism, mostly cleared by the kidneys. High blood levels (hyperuricemia) raise gout risk and may reflect reduced excretion.
