**中文：** 尿酸盐结晶沉积引起的炎性关节炎，常突然剧痛、红肿，多见于足拇趾关节。多与高尿酸血症相关；饮酒、高嘌呤饮食等可诱发。

**English:** Inflammatory arthritis from urate crystals—sudden pain and swelling, often in the big toe. Linked to high uric acid; alcohol and purine-rich meals can trigger flares.
