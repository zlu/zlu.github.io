**中文：** 环绕眼裂的面部表情肌，负责闭眼与眨眼。其细小肌束不自主颤动，是眼睑肌纤维颤搐（眼皮跳）的主要解剖基础。

**English:** Circumorbital muscle that closes the eyelids and blinks. Fine fasciculations in its fibers are the usual substrate of eyelid myokymia.
