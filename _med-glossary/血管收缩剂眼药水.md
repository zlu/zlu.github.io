**中文：** 通过收缩结膜浅表血管暂时减轻“红眼”的滴眼液。停药后可出现反跳性充血，不能治疗干眼病因，反复眼不适时不宜依赖。

**English:** Drops that whiten the eye by constricting conjunctival vessels. Rebound redness is common; they do not treat dry eye and should not be used for recurring eye discomfort.
