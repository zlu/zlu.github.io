**中文：** 注视灯光时看到彩虹样光圈或晕轮。急性闭角型青光眼眼压骤升时可出现，常同时有视物模糊、眼痛、眼红或恶心；出现此类危险信号应尽快就医。

**English:** Rainbow rings or halos around lights. Classic with acute IOP spikes in angle-closure glaucoma, often with blur, eye pain, redness, or nausea—seek urgent eye care if this appears.
