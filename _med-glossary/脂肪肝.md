**中文：** 肝细胞内脂肪过度堆积；与肥胖、代谢综合征密切相关，少数可进展为炎症与纤维化。

**English:** Excess fat in liver cells; tied to obesity/metabolic syndrome; some progress to steatohepatitis and fibrosis.
