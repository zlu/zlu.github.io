**中文：** 用于湿润、润滑眼表的眼药水，是干眼的一线居家处理。频繁使用者宜选无防腐剂（单支独立包装）剂型；冷藏后滴用有时更舒适。不要用“去红血丝”类收缩血管滴眼液代替。

**English:** Lubricating drops that supplement the tear film—first-line home care for dry eye. Prefer preservative-free unit-dose vials if used often; refrigerated drops can feel soothing. Do not substitute redness-relieving vasoconstrictor drops.
