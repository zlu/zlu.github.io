**中文：** 单位时间内身体消耗能量的快慢。安静空腹时的最低消耗称基础代谢率（BMR）；全天总消耗还包括活动与食物热效应。

**English:** How quickly the body uses energy. BMR is resting minimum burn; total daily expenditure also includes activity and digestion.
