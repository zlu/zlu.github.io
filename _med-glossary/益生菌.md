**中文：** 摄入足够数量后对健康有益的活微生物（如乳杆菌、双歧杆菌），见于酸奶、发酵食品及补充剂。效果因菌株与剂量而异。

**English:** Live microbes that benefit the host in adequate amounts (e.g., lactobacilli, bifidobacteria)—in yogurt, fermented foods, or supplements. Strain and dose matter.
