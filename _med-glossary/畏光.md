**中文：** 对正常光线感到不适甚至疼痛。角膜表层病变、眼内炎症、偏头痛及急性闭角型青光眼等均可出现；眼痛伴明显畏光、眼红时应尽快就诊。

**English:** Discomfort or pain from ordinary light. Corneal surface disease, intraocular inflammation, migraine, and acute angle closure can cause it; eye pain plus marked photophobia and redness warrants prompt care.
