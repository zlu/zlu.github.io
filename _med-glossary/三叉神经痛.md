**中文：** 三叉神经分布区突发、闪电样锐痛，多单侧；典型可持续数秒至两分钟。眼支轻微异常放电时，也可只表现为眼周定点、瞬间即逝的刺痛，未必达到典型三叉神经痛强度。

**English:** Sudden, lightning-like pain in a trigeminal distribution, usually unilateral. Mild ophthalmic-branch (V1) irritation can feel like a pinpoint jab around the eye lasting only a second, short of classic trigeminal neuralgia.
