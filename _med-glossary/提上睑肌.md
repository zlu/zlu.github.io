**中文：** 负责上提上眼睑的主要肌肉。疲劳或局部微小肌束活动时，有人会感觉上眼睑与眼球之间出现短暂刺痛或挤压感。

**English:** Main muscle that elevates the upper eyelid. Fatigue or tiny fasciculations can be felt as a brief jab or pressure between the upper lid and the globe.
