**中文：** 大体积血小板占全部血小板的比例，英文缩写常见为 P-LCR。多与血小板计数、平均血小板体积等一起出现在血常规报告中。

**English:** Platelet large cell ratio (P-LCR): the share of larger platelets. Usually reported with platelet count and related platelet indices on a CBC.
