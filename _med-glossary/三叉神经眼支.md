**中文：** 三叉神经第一支（V1），感觉支配额部、上眼睑、结膜与角膜等。该支短暂异常放电，可造成眼睑与眼球交界处定点、瞬间消失的刺痛。

**English:** First division of the trigeminal nerve (V1); sensory supply to forehead, upper eyelid, conjunctiva, and cornea. Brief ectopic firing can cause a pinpoint stab at the lid–globe junction that vanishes in a second.
