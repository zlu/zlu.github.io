**中文：** 携氧的主要血细胞。计数过低常见于贫血或失血，过高可见于缺氧代偿或浓缩。

**English:** Oxygen-carrying red blood cells (RBC). Low counts often track anemia or blood loss; high counts may reflect hypoxia compensation or hemoconcentration.
