**BM25** is a classic ranking function for [sparse retrieval](/ai-glossary/sparse-retrieval/) over bag-of-words indexes. It saturates term frequency, down-weights very common terms (IDF), and length-normalizes documents—usually beating plain TF-IDF in practice.

### Why it matters
- Strong lexical baseline: exact keywords, IDs, rare proper nouns
- Cheap and explainable compared with embedding search
- Default first-stage retriever in many search stacks

### Relation to dense retrieval
[Dense retrieval](/ai-glossary/dense-retrieval/) catches paraphrases BM25 misses; BM25 catches literal matches embeddings blur. [Hybrid search](/ai-glossary/hybrid-search/) (BM25 + vectors, then fuse/rerank) is often the pragmatic RAG default. Tune $k_1$ and $b$ lightly; bigger wins usually come from better analyzers, fields, and chunking—not exotic BM25 variants.
