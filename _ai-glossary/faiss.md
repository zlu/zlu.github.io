**FAISS** (Facebook AI Similarity Search) is a library of exact and approximate vector search indexes on CPU/GPU. It is a common building block under custom RAG stacks and some vector DBs.

Choose IVF/HNSW/PQ configs based on scale, memory, and recall targets.
