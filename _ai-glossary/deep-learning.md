**Deep learning** is [machine learning](/ai-glossary/machine-learning/) with multi-layer [neural networks](/ai-glossary/neural-network/) that learn hierarchical representations from data, reducing hand-built features for many perception and language tasks. Depth plus nonlinearity lets models compose simple features into complex ones ([representation learning](/ai-glossary/representation-learning/)).

### Why it matters
- Backbone of modern vision, speech, and LLMs
- Progress tracked closely with scale: data, parameters, and compute
- Architectures ([CNNs](/ai-glossary/convolutional-neural-network/), Transformers) matter as much as raw size

### How it shows up
Train with [backpropagation](/ai-glossary/backpropagation/) and an [optimizer](/ai-glossary/optimizer/); serve with forward passes only. For practitioners, “deep learning” usually means GPU/TPU training, automatic differentiation, and architecture families—not shallow linear models. LLMs are deep learning at extreme scale with [Transformer](/ai-glossary/transformer/) blocks.
