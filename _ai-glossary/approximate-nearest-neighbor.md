**Approximate nearest neighbor (ANN)** algorithms find mostly-the-nearest vectors much faster than exact search, using indexes like HNSW, IVF, or LSH.

Recall/latency trade-offs are tunable; evaluate with your embedding distribution, not synthetic spheres alone.
