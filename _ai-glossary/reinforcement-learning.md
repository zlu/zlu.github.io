title: Reinforcement Learning
synonyms:
  - RL
related:
  - RLHF
  - Policy
---
**Reinforcement learning** trains an agent to maximize expected reward through interaction with an environment. Policies map states to actions; learning uses returns, advantages, and exploration.

In LLMs, RL appears in RLHF/RLAIF-style alignment where the “environment” is a preference or reward model over generated text.
