**How does online CS tutoring work?**

Main classroom apps: **ClassIn** or **Tencent Meeting** — please install beforehand. Also available: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Meet, WeChat video. Send the syllabus, code, assignment, or error log ahead of time; we screen-share, explain bilingually, and debug together.

**Why book directly?**

Intake, prep, and teaching are the same person. You **WeChat the tutor directly**. **No middleman fee**, and no sales handoff to someone else.

**How much does it cost? Is there a middleman fee?**

No middleman fee. Pricing depends on topic and session length; you pay the person teaching. Short trials are available. Message on WeChat for a quote.

**Do you write assignments, papers, or take exams?**

No. I explain, debug, and help structure reports/projects. You write and submit your own work. That protects integrity and builds skills that stick.

**My English is weak — can I still keep up?**

Yes. Bilingual teaching: Chinese for intuition, English for the terms exams and assignments require. Fast lectures and heavy accents are common reasons students book.

**Can you help with reports or a thesis?**

Yes — topic discussion, outline, experiment design, figures, and structure. I do not ghostwrite the text or submit for you. Bring your draft or data.

**What should I send before the first lesson?**

School, course code, deadline, plus the brief / code / errors / slide screenshots. Better materials = a more useful first hour.
